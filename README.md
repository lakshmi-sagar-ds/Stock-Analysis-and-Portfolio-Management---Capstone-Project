# 📈 Stock Analysis & Portfolio Management for Long-Term Investment Planning

## ✅ Objective  
This capstone project aimed to design a **stable, low-to-moderate risk investment portfolio** for a real-world client, **Ms. Alexandra Kolishnyic**, a college student with a long-term goal of funding an NGO after graduation.

The objective was to:
- Preserve capital
- Achieve **moderate, steady growth**
- Maintain transparency and explainability
- Manage downside risk over a **4–6 year investment horizon**

The portfolio strategy was designed using **data-driven stock analysis, financial risk metrics, and time-series forecasting**, with the **S&P 500 as a benchmark**.

---

## 👤 Understanding the Client & Business Context  

Client profile was established using the **5W + How framework**:

- **Investment Horizon:** 4–6 years (college duration)
- **Risk Appetite:** Low to moderate
- **Primary Goal:** Capital preservation with steady growth
- **Secondary Goal:** Outperform savings accounts and market benchmark
- **Funds Source:** Personal savings supported by parental contributions
- **Constraints:** High transparency, low volatility, explainable decisions

The portfolio needed to be **educational, conservative, and resilient**, not speculative.

---

## 📊 About the Data  

- **Market:** US equities (NYSE-listed stocks)
- **Time Period:** 2010–2020 (historical analysis) with projections beyond
- **Data Type:** Daily stock prices and returns
- **Industries Covered:**
  - Technology
  - Healthcare
  - Finance
  - Aviation
- **Benchmark:** S&P 500 Index

Stocks were chosen to represent **large-cap, stable companies** with sufficient liquidity.

---

## Stock Selection Methodology  

Stocks were shortlisted using a **multi-metric financial evaluation framework**, combining:

- **Beta** – market sensitivity (lower preferred)
- **CAPM Expected Return**
- **Actual Historical Returns**
- **Jensen’s Alpha** – excess return beyond expectation
- **Annualised Volatility**
- **Sharpe Ratio** – return per unit of risk

Each stock received a **weighted composite score**, balancing return and risk rather than chasing performance alone.

---

## Portfolio Construction  

The final portfolio was diversified across **six large-cap stocks**:

- Amazon (Technology)
- Apple (Technology)
- Alphabet (Technology)
- Johnson & Johnson (Healthcare)
- Merck & Co. (Healthcare)
- Alaska Air Group (Aviation)

Key principles applied:
- Sector diversification to reduce concentration risk
- Preference for **low beta and stable volatility**
- Equal-weighted allocation for simplicity and transparency

---

## 📈 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)  

Extensive EDA was performed to understand behaviour across stocks and sectors:

- **Daily return distributions** highlighted variability across industries
- **Volatility analysis** showed technology stocks had higher dispersion than healthcare
- **Correlation matrices** revealed:
  - Strong intra-sector correlations
  - Diversification benefits across sectors
- **Trading volume trends** indicated liquidity stability across years
- **Risk vs return plots** helped identify efficient candidates

Visualisations were used extensively to ensure explainability.

---

## Time Series Analysis & Forecasting  

- Daily and log returns were analysed to capture long-term behaviour
- Time-series forecasting models were applied to individual stocks
- Portfolio-level projections were generated by aggregating stock forecasts
- Cumulative portfolio value was projected forward to assess the long-term growth trajectory

The forecasting approach focused on **trend continuity**, not speculative price targets.

---

## Risk Management Strategy  

Risk was managed using multiple layers:

- **Low beta selection** to reduce market sensitivity
- **Volatility control** through diversification
- **Positive Jensen’s Alpha** to ensure excess return justification
- **Sharpe Ratio tracking** to ensure risk-adjusted performance

A separate **contingency fund** was recommended outside the portfolio for emergencies.

---

## Performance Expectations  

Based on historical behaviour and forecasts:

- Portfolio showed **steadier growth** than the S&P 500
- Exhibited **lower volatility and fewer sharp drawdowns**
- Expected to outperform savings instruments while maintaining safety
- Aligned with the client’s NGO funding timeline (4–6 years)

---

## Recommendations  

- Maintain a **long-term holding strategy**
- Avoid emotional or short-term rebalancing
- Conduct **quarterly performance reviews**
- Perform an **annual deep review** to realign with financial goals
- Keep emergency funds outside equity investments
